## The problem

Global product delivery needed a dedicated team to turn raw market data into clean, harmonized data for each region, with the same quality standards everywhere.

## What I did

- Built the India analytics team from scratch.
- Set up the QA framework, quality standards and ways of working with research, commercial and engineering teams in other time zones.
- Work hands-on with the analysts on extracting, transforming and loading the data (SQL, Python, dbt, Snowflake, BigQuery).
- Moved quality checks on newly collected data from the research teams to the data team. Before, those checks waited on research teams busy with other priorities and quarterly releases, sometimes for months, so data sat unpublished until someone suddenly needed it.

## The result

- A team of 4 analysts now maintains harmonized data across Aurora's global markets.
- Turnaround from data collection to publication is much faster now that the data team owns quality checks, and we're still working to make it faster.
