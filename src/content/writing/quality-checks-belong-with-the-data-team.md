title: Quality checks belong with the data team
description: Why moving quality checks on newly collected data from research teams to the data team made our data ship much faster.

For a long time, quality checks on newly collected market data sat with the research teams who would eventually use it. On paper that made sense. They knew the markets best, so they were best placed to judge whether a price series looked right.

In practice, the checks waited. Research teams had their own priorities and quarterly releases, and a batch of new data was rarely the most urgent thing on their desk. Sometimes it waited for months. The data sat unpublished until someone suddenly needed it, and then everyone was in a hurry.

## What we changed

We moved those checks into the data team. The team that collects and harmonizes the data now also checks it, against a shared QA framework with the same standards for every region. Research teams still review and approve data before release, but they are no longer the queue that new data waits in.

## Why it works

- **The people closest to the data check it first.** The analysts who load a series already know its sources, its gaps and its quirks.
- **Checks run on the data team's schedule.** Quality work stops competing with research deadlines.
- **Standards stay the same everywhere.** One framework, applied by one team, means a market in Asia Pacific is checked the same way as one in Europe.

## The result

Turnaround from collection to publication is much faster, and we are still working to make it faster. The bigger change is cultural: data quality is now part of shipping data, not a favour someone does later.
